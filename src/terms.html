What Virus Vigil is

A tool that looks for signs of malware, spy apps, scams and weak security settings, and explains what it finds in plain words. It checks against public malware knowledge (listed on the Licences and credits page) and against its own rules.

What it cannot promise

  • No security tool catches everything. New malware appears every day, and a brand-new threat may not be in any list yet. A clean result means nothing was found in what was checked. It does not mean your device is safe.
  • Results can be wrong in both directions: a harmless file may be flagged, and a harmful one may be missed.

Changes it makes

On a laptop, Virus Vigil changes a security setting only after you approve it (and Windows asks you again), and every change it makes can be undone from the app. It never deletes, moves or uploads your files.

On a phone, Android does not let an app change these settings itself. Virus Vigil opens the right Settings page, or Android's own Uninstall box for an app the check found, and you make every change. Nothing is removed unless you confirm it in Android's box.
